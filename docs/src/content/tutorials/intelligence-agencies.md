title: Intelligence Agencies
description: How to use intelligence agencies and Targeted Operations in Millennium Dawn OEM
---

Intelligence agencies, operatives, and operations come from the La Résistance expansion and work as they do in the base game. Millennium Dawn adds its own agency upgrades and operations. Agency upgrades are selected manually in the Intelligence Agency window.

## Targeted Operations in OEM

Open **Targeted Ops** in **Intelligence Agency**. Without an agency, use **Open National Intelligence** in Decisions. The **Operations** tab opens first. Select a person or organization, use **Collection** to build a case, then return to **Operations** to request authorization, commit the operation, or confirm its result. The **Dossier** tab is for reference, not launch. Counter-Terror remains separate in International Systems.

- **Remote strike and capture raid:** Begin preparation in National Intelligence, then launch the authorized raid from the raid map.
- **Covert action, rendition, partner operations, Russia's special operation, and facility sabotage:** With an agency and a foreign host, begin preparation in National Intelligence. Select the authorized host in the Agency panel and prepare **Execute Authorized Case** with one operative, 35% network strength, and two units of C&C equipment before cost modifiers. Without an agency, or for a domestic case, these methods keep their timed route. The case must still be valid at completion.
- **Political stability:** In the Agency Operations list, **Collect Political Intelligence** sends the initiating country a private report about the country investigated. It does not authorize action against a person.
- **Aftermath:** Use **Custody**, **Archive**, and **Security** in National Intelligence. An assassination opens a separate investigation in the victim country's service. The report is ready after 14 days and identifies the other country only if there is credible or confirmed evidence.

Use **Liaison** below the top bar to request a report on a known subject from another faction member. The member can decline. Faction liaison does not give the faction authority to start a national operation.

Set the **Targeted Operations** game rule to **Off** to disable these OEM features. This does not disable ordinary Agency operations or Counter-Terror.
