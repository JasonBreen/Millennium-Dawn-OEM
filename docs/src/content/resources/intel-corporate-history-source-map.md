## Cross-company ownership boundaries

Intel's chain does not duplicate milestones owned by other corporate histories and never writes their state. The Apple chain owns the Apple-Intel transition and Apple Silicon transition; the Micron chain owns IM Flash; Taiwan's PC chain owns the VIA cross-license settlement. IBM, NVIDIA, TSMC, and ATI/AMD outcomes are also external state. Intel reads the following flags only as AI-choice context:

These flags do not gate event delivery, silent reconstruction, or terminal resolution.
