# Polish Industrial Sovereignty source register and implementation contract

## Scope and non-goals

This Tier-1 Corporate History chain models Polish state policy. It does not require,
read, or write the Korean, German, United States, Ukrainian, Rail Baltica, energy,
MIO, focus-tree, or legacy-economic systems. It makes no claim that procurement,
assembly, licensing, maintenance, delivery, or interoperability is technology transfer
unless the cited record says so.

The user-supplied _Poland//Stack: The Polish Tech Industry, 2000-2026_ report (August 2026) is used as a secondary synthesis. Its evidence boundary and source index inform the
2000 baseline, the EU/programme, service-export, public-stack, and diffusion framing below;
its interpretive “SIGNAL” passages are not used as factual event anchors.

## Event and ownership inventory

- Namespace: `POL_industrial_sovereignty_events`; visible events `.1` through `.15`; hidden reconstruction anchor `.90`.
- State variables: domestic control, technological depth, export capacity, systems integration, and supply resilience. All are initialized once and clamped to `0..10` after every mutation.
- The startup dispatcher owns Full scheduling and Outcomes Only reconstruction. Yearly effects own only `POL_corporate_trigger_year_<year>` wrappers. The existing `on_monthly_POL` owns the Outcomes Only retry driver.
- Full schedules only the valid current start year and exposes historical policy choices. Outcomes Only uses the silent ladder and never replays PP or economic rewards. Disabled creates no Polish Industrial Sovereignty state.
- The terminal resolver first removes every terminal idea and then grants exactly one civil-war-persistent outcome. Full exposes each qualified outcome plus the viable fallback; Outcomes Only resolves the European Production Hub.
