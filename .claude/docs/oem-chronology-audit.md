# OEM Chronology and Dependency Audit

## Scope and method

This audit covers every fork-owned OEM and corporate-history namespace present on
the audited branch. It compares each visible event's historical milestone with
its annual or direct caller, predecessor markers, reconstruction threshold, and
next dependent event. A `days` value is the zero-based offset from January 1, so
leap years are accounted for when converting offsets to dates.

`PASS` means the audited caller and reconstruction ladder preserve the intended
relative chronology and every required predecessor is produced before it is
read. It does not claim Hearts of Iron IV runtime testing.

## Dependency invariants

- An owner decision sets its path marker before notifying a receiver. IBM owns
  the PC disposition; Lenovo reacts and never writes IBM state.
- Mutually exclusive Sun outcomes remain mutually exclusive. Oracle's Sun event
  requires the Oracle path, while Oracle's own later chronology can advance past
  a terminal non-Oracle Sun result without pretending Oracle acquired Sun.
- Google extension and whole-chain Oracle catch-up resolve only the earliest
  eligible missing event. One 400-day timed pending flag per chain prevents a
  queued event from being added again after save/reload.
- Late Full-mode starts restore Google's event-14 distribution marker when
  needed. Oracle starts visibly at `.1`, follows the reconstructed Sun path, and
  leaves every Oracle option player-owned.
- IBM reconstruction uses the start-year-specific delay from its hidden day-two
  anchor, preserving the March 2005 CFIUS and October 2007 follow-up dates. Its
  Lenovo notification has a timed pending guard and monthly retry before the May
  2005 close; later reconstruction marks the Lenovo path chosen and cannot reopen
  the pre-acquisition decision.
- Reconstruction uses the same historical ordering as live scheduling. A
  threshold is the day before or the exact inclusive boundary required by the
  script's `date >` semantics.
- Foxconn live yearly dates were not changed. Only stale year-end
  reconstruction thresholds were aligned with their already-scheduled events.
