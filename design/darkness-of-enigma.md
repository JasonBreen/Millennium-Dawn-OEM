## Authorization and trials

The literal USA tag, Corporate History Full, Fictional Content Yes, Experimental
Neural Aviation Enabled, and By Blood Alone are required. The category appears
during 2026-2034, or after a charter has started. A new charter requires computing_5,
avionics_3, and artificial_intelligence_8.

1. Choose public funding for 50 Political Power, or classified funding for 25
   Political Power and 20 Exposure.
2. Pay 75 Political Power for adult-volunteer trials. Public trials take 90 days;
   classified trials take 60.
3. The authored incident records one test-pilot death and 10 Exposure. Investigate
   independently for 50 Political Power; falsify the report for 30 Exposure; impose
   coercive medical-discharge terms for 25 Political Power, 10 Air Experience, and
   40 Exposure; or cancel for 1% Stability.
4. Pay 25 Political Power for a 60-day review.
5. Publish for 25 Political Power and 15 Air Experience, losing 1% Stability below
   40 Exposure or 4% at 40 or more, then reducing Exposure by 10. Alternatively,
   seal the record for 10 Air Experience and 20 Exposure, or cancel for 2% Stability.

Publishing or sealing authorizes development, not technology completion. Neither
choice grants aircraft. Descriptions preserve the different evidence paths.

## Hearings and engineer departures

An automatic 180-day decision mission adds five Exposure with public funding or
ten with classified funding, plus ten while at war, capped at 100. Exposure is then
the percentage chance of a first leak; 100 guarantees it. A 50-Political-Power
consent/data audit reduces Exposure by twenty, with a 180-day cooldown.

A completed American project also opens a first hearing at 60 or more Exposure:

- An independently investigated program below 60 Exposure can accept oversight
  for 100 Political Power and 2% Stability. Funding becomes public, Exposure becomes
  ten, and new project starts are held for 180 days.
- A cover-up costs 50 Political Power and 3% Stability, adds twenty Exposure, and
  guarantees a second leak at the next 180-day scrutiny cycle.
- Cancellation ends authorization.

The second leak offers federal oversight for 150 Political Power and 8% Stability,
with public funding, twenty Exposure, and a 365-day hold on new starts, or
cancellation. Leak count and the original trial record survive both outcomes.

Oversight or cancellation triggers one engineer decision. An audited Northrop
transfer costs 25 Political Power and gives one 20% avionics_4 research bonus, or
ten Air Experience if already researched. Retention costs 50 Political Power and
gives five Air Experience but adds fifteen Exposure. Unmanaged departure adds
twenty Exposure.

At 60 or more Exposure, without Passive Defense, departures offer CHI incomplete
data. CHI can pay 75 Political Power to authorize domestic reconstruction or refuse.
Both sides require all Enigma feature gates. Acceptance transfers neither American
research nor NIGHTMARE. Reconstruction unlocks only the limited cockpit.

Cancellation and oversight holds do not erase completed research, remove aircraft,
or forcibly stop already-running native laboratory work. They control authorization
for new starts. Player-facing text makes this distinction explicit.
